Herbalist's Companion — Support

Updated October 9, 2026

Herbalist's Companion is a materia medica of Western herbs and a bench for building herbal formulas by role. This page says how to reach us and how to do the few things people most often ask about.

Reaching us

  • From inside the app. Open Settings (the mark at the top of the Herbs screen) and use the feedback form at the foot of the page. It works whether or not you are signed in.
  • By email. jared@penumbral.org. Say which phone you use and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Your account

  • Signing in. With an email address and password, with Google, or on iPhone and iPad with Apple. Browsing the herbs and building a formula need no account; saving one does.
  • A forgotten password. On the sign-in screen, choose to reset it and follow the link we email you.
  • Deleting your account. Sign in, open Settings and tap Delete Account. Everything saved to the account is removed at once and cannot be recovered. If you no longer have the app, email us from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days. The privacy policy says exactly what is removed.

A formula someone published

If a published formula is unsafe, abusive or otherwise wrong for the app, open it, sign in and tap Report this formula. A person reads every report.

What the app is not

Herbalist's Companion records what herbal tradition holds about a plant. It is a reference for study and practice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, it is not medical advice, and it is no substitute for a qualified practitioner. Talk to one before taking any herb for a health condition, if you are pregnant or nursing, or if you take any medicine.
