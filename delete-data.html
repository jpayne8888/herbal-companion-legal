Herbalist’s Companion — Delete your data

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Herbalist’s Companion account and everything stored with it. There are two routes: from inside the app, and by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Herbalist’s Companion.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Your account and every formula saved to it are removed.

Delete by email

  1. Email jared@penumbral.org from the email address associated with your account, so the request can be matched to it.
  2. State that you are requesting deletion of your Herbalist’s Companion account.

Your account and all saved formulas will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get a confirmation by email once it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data What happens
Email address Deleted
Name and profile photo (only if you signed in with Google) Deleted
Saved formulas and any notes on them Deleted
Feedback you sent from inside the app Deleted

In short: everything tied to your account goes. Nothing about you is kept back.

Retention

Nothing is retained after deletion except where the law requires it.

Deleted data can persist for a short period in our hosting provider’s routine backup rotation, which ages out within 30 days. Those backups are not used to restore deleted accounts.

The herb catalogue itself is reference content and holds nothing about you, so it is unaffected either way.

Questions

Email jared@penumbral.org. The full privacy policy covers what is collected and why.
