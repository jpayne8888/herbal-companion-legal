Herbalist’s Companion: Delete your data

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Herbalist’s Companion account and everything stored with it. There are two routes: from inside the app, and by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Herbalist’s Companion and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings button at the top right of the Home screen, or of the Herbs screen if the app opens on Herbs.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account again to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Your account and every formula saved to it are removed.

Delete by email

  1. Email jared@penumbral.org from the email address associated with your account, so the request can be matched to it.
  2. State that you are requesting deletion of your Herbalist’s Companion account.

Your account and all saved formulas will be deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data What happens
Email address Deleted
Your name (only if you used “Continue with Apple” on Android and left a name there; Google gives us only your email address) Deleted
Saved formulas and their notes Deleted
Your marks and notes on herbs Deleted
Formulas you published, their photos, and their history Deleted
The record of when each formula was first published Deleted
The makers you hide Deleted
Reports filed under your account Deleted
Feedback sent while signed in Deleted
Feedback sent while signed out Carries no identity, so it cannot be traced to you or found to delete. Nothing deletes it automatically.
Moderation records: of moderation actions, of automatic photo refusals, and of hidden makers Kept by account number, with no email address and no photo, for as long as we operate the app

Retention

What is deleted, and the little that is kept, is described under “Data retention” in the privacy policy.

We keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider’s routine backup rotation, which ages out within 30 days. Server logs are kept for a limited period, as described under “Information collected automatically” in the privacy policy.

The herb catalogue itself is reference content and holds nothing about you, so it is unaffected either way.

Questions

Email jared@penumbral.org. The full privacy policy covers what is collected and why.
