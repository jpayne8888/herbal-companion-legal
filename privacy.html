Herbalist's Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Herbalist's Companion ("the app", "we", "us") is an herbal formula-building app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to provide the app's core features:

  • Email address — collected when you create an account, used to sign you in and to let you reset your password.
  • Your email address from Google or Apple, if you use "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple": as provided by that company's sign-in service. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the relay address Apple gives us and never your real one. From Google we ask for nothing else (not your name, not your photo), and we use the address only to create and identify your account.
  • Your name, if you use "Continue with Apple" on an Android device and choose to share it: on Android, Apple's sign-in page shows the name on your Apple account and lets you change it before you continue. Whatever name you leave there is passed to us the first time you sign in this way and is stored with your account. The app does not show it or use it for anything, and it is deleted with your account. On iPhone and iPad we do not ask Apple for your name.
  • An account identifier — a random number our database assigns to your account, which ties your saved work to you.
  • Formula data you save — the herb formulas, notes, and preparation details you build and choose to save. Saving a formula needs an account, and a saved formula is stored with it. A formula you are still building on the bench stays on your device until you save it.
  • Your notes on herbs — if you sign in, the marks you make on a herb (read, kept) and the note you write about it are stored with your account. Signed out, they stay on your device, and in your device's own backup if you use one; we never see them. If you later sign in on that device, the marks and notes you made there are moved into your account so they are not lost, as are any formulas an earlier version of the app saved on the device.
  • Feedback you send from Settings — the message, its category, and the version of the app you sent it from. If you are signed in it is filed under your account; if not, it carries no identity at all.
  • Reports you make — if you report a published formula, your report and the reason you give are filed under your account so that we can act on it.
  • Formulas you choose to publish — see "Shared formulas" below for exactly what a published formula contains, who can see it, and how to take it back.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device advertising ID, or any analytics/usage tracking. The app reads your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a formula you are publishing (see "Photos on a shared formula" below), and never otherwise. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs as of this policy's effective date. Subscriptions are built into the app but switched off in this release; the subscription service is named under "Third parties" below so that nothing in the app is undisclosed, and this policy will be updated before it is switched on.

Shared formulas

You can choose to publish a formula so that other people can open it. Nothing is published unless you tap Publish, and the screen shows you exactly what will become visible. A published formula contains: its name and preparation type, a tincture's alcohol percentage, the herbs with their roles and proportions, the computed energetic, taste and action profile, any tags, the conditions you file it under, and what you write for it on the publish screen (its description, its preparation instructions and its Shared Notes), any photos you add, and your username. The private notes you keep on a formula in your Library are never published. A published formula never contains your herb notebook, your email address or your account identity.

Its history. When you change a published formula, the version it replaces is kept with it, so that its changes over time can be shown to you. Only you can read that history. It is deleted with the formula when you withdraw it or delete your account.

Photos on a shared formula

You may add up to six photos of plants, plant material or the making to a formula you publish. Before a photo is added, your phone checks it for faces using the face detection built into your phone's own operating system (Apple's Vision framework on iPhone and iPad, Android's own face detector on Android). That check runs entirely on your device; the photo is not sent anywhere to be checked, and a photo with a face, or one the phone cannot check, is not added. The location and other camera details are removed from every photo on your device before it is uploaded. Photos are stored privately with your published formula and shown to people who open it. They are deleted when you remove them, when you withdraw the formula, or when you delete your account.

Checking shared photos. When you choose or take a photo for a shared formula, and again after it is uploaded unless it was already checked as you chose it, and before anyone but you can see it, our server sends it to Google Cloud Vision, a service that checks it on our behalf for sexual, violent or otherwise explicit content and for a human face. The photo is sent to Google only for that check. Google's terms for this service say that the photo is processed in memory and not stored, that Google does not use it to train or improve its products, and that Google keeps a temporary log of some details about each request (not the photo) to run the service and prevent abuse (Cloud Vision data usage). The face check only asks whether a face is present. It does not recognize or identify anyone, and we keep nothing about any face: no measurements, no outline, no score. We keep Google's answer on explicit content and, if a photo is refused, the reason. When a photo passes as you choose it, we keep a short fingerprint of it for a day: a code worked out from the photo, which cannot be turned back into it, and not the photo itself. It lets the same photo pass after upload without being sent to Google a second time. A photo refused as you choose it is not kept by us at all and is not added to your formula; one refused after upload is deleted and taken off your formula. Either way the app tells you so. Until the check passes, a photo is visible to you alone.

The terms you accept. The first time you publish a formula, the app shows the rules for shared formulas and asks you to agree to them. We keep a record that your account agreed, which version of the terms it was, and when, so we do not ask again until the terms change.

Who can see it. At present a published formula can be found by anyone using the app: the Discover tab searches every published formula by the herbs in it, and anyone who has a formula's link can open it too. The publish screen says this before you confirm. The app also has a community section, the Commons, where published formulas are listed for every reader to find; it is not yet open. When it opens, formulas that are published at that time are listed there, and the publish screen tells you this before you confirm. You can unpublish a formula at any time from your Library, after which its link no longer opens it and it is not listed.

Demonstration links. A formula published before 12 September 2026 may carry a link to a video of the preparation that its maker had posted on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Vimeo; the publish screen no longer offers this. Where such a link exists we store the address and nothing else. The app does not contact those services on your behalf or on a reader's; a reader who taps the link leaves the app and is then subject to that service's own privacy policy.

Reports and removal. Every published formula carries a Report button for anyone signed in. Reports are read only by us, never by the person reported, and are reviewed by a person. A formula that is unsafe, unlawful, abusive, misleading about what an herb will do, or otherwise not what the app is for is removed, with its photos. An account that publishes such a formula may be suspended, which stops it signing in and publishing, and repeated or serious cases lead to the account being closed. We also look over newly published formulas and their photos ourselves, without waiting for a report, and remove any that break these rules. Every shared photo is also checked automatically before anyone else can see it, on your phone for faces and by Google Cloud Vision for explicit content and faces (both described above); there is no other automated moderation. If you believe something of yours was removed in error, contact us at the address below.

Hiding a formula or a maker. You can hide a published formula, or hide its maker so that none of their formulas appear for you. The maker is not told. A hidden formula is remembered on your device only. If you are signed in, the makers you hide are kept with your account, so that their formulas are left out before they reach your device, and that list entry is deleted when you show the maker again or delete your account. If you are signed out, the makers you hide are remembered on your device only. Each time a signed-in reader hides a maker we also keep a record of it by account number (the maker's and the reader's, with the time, and nothing else), so that we can see when several readers hide the same maker. That record is kept after the maker is shown again.

How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store and sync your saved formulas across your devices.
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. password reset, email confirmation) — we do not send marketing email.
  • To read the feedback you send and fix what it reports.
  • To review reports about published formulas and remove what breaks the rules above.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Where your data is stored

Account and formula data is stored using Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider that hosts our database and handles authentication. Supabase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your saved formulas is restricted at the database level so that only your authenticated account can read or write them.

Like any web service, our provider's servers record the IP address and time of each request the app makes to them, signed in or not, in logs kept for a limited period for security and to diagnose faults. We do not use those logs to work out where you are or to build any profile of you.

On your device, your sign-in session is encrypted and stored using your operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), not in plain text.

Supabase hosts our database in Northern Virginia, United States (AWS us-east-1). If you use the app from outside the United States, your account and formulas are transferred there in order to provide the service. For users in the UK or the European Economic Area, that transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses contained in Supabase's data processing addendum, which forms part of our agreement with them as our processor.

Third parties the app communicates with

These are every external service the app contacts, and the one our server contacts with your content (Google Cloud Vision). There are no others, and there is no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, acting as our processor. Receives everything listed under "Information we collect" above, because it is where that information is stored. It does not use your data for its own purposes. (privacy policy)
  • Google Cloud Vision: contacted by our server, not by the app, and only for photos you add to a formula you publish. It receives each such photo to check it, as described under "Checking shared photos" above, acting on our behalf. (data usage)
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google tells us your email address, and Google knows that you signed in to this app. We ask Google for nothing else and send it nothing else. (privacy policy)
  • Sign in with Apple: only if you choose "Continue with Apple". Apple tells us your email address, or a relay address if you choose to hide yours, and Apple knows that you signed in to this app. On iPhone and iPad we ask Apple for nothing else. On Android, where Apple's sign-in runs as a web page, Apple also passes the name you give it there, if you leave one, and we keep it with your account. We send Apple nothing else. (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — built in, switched off in this release. When subscriptions are offered, it handles the purchase through Google Play or the App Store and receives an app-generated identifier for your account and your purchase state; it never receives your email address. It is not contacted while subscriptions are off. (privacy policy)
  • Expo (EAS Update) — delivers fixes to the app between store releases. Each time the app launches it asks Expo whether a newer update exists for its version. That request tells Expo your device platform, the app's version, your IP address, and a random identifier created for that installation. It carries no account information and nothing you have written, and it happens whether or not you are signed in. Every update is signed by us, and the app refuses one that is not. Version 1.1 of the app does not make this request; versions released before October 2026 did, without the signature check. (privacy policy)
  • Google Play — distributes the app on Android and may collect installation and crash diagnostics under its own terms, independently of anything the app does. (privacy policy)
  • Apple (the App Store and TestFlight) — distributes the app on iPhone and iPad and may collect installation and crash diagnostics under its own terms, where you have agreed to share them with developers. (privacy policy)

How long we keep your data

Your account and formulas are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account — in the app, or by emailing us — the account and everything saved to it are removed: your formulas, your herb notes, the formulas you published, and the feedback and reports filed under your account. Nothing is retained afterwards except where the law requires it. Feedback sent while signed out carries no identity, so it cannot be traced to you or found to delete; it is kept until we have dealt with it. We keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider's routine backup rotation, which ages out within 30 days.

Why we are allowed to hold it

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the legal basis for processing your email address and your saved formulas is performance of a contract — you asked for an account and for your formulas to be stored, and neither is possible without them. Feedback is processed because you chose to send it. Reports, and the short-lived server logs described above, are processed in our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and working. There is no other purpose. You have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Your choices and rights

  • Access — you can view all your saved formulas at any time in the app's Library tab.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and everything saved to it at any time from Settings → Delete Account while signed in. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Sign out — you can sign out of your account at any time from Settings without deleting your data.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can still request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at the address below from the email address associated with your account. State that you're requesting account deletion. We will delete your account and all saved formulas within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

Children's privacy

Herbalist's Companion is made for adults, and you must be 18 or older to use it. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
