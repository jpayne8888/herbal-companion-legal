Herbalist's Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Herbalist's Companion ("the app", "we", "us") is a herbal formula-building app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to provide the app's core features:

  • Email address — collected when you create an account, used to sign you in and to let you reset your password.
  • Your email address from Google, if you use "Continue with Google" — as provided by Google's sign-in service. We ask Google for nothing else, and we use it only to create and identify your account.
  • Formula data you create — the herb formulas, notes, and preparation details you build and choose to save. This is only stored with your account if you're signed in and tap Save; signed out, it stays on your device.
  • Your notes on herbs — if you sign in, the marks you make on a herb (read, kept) and the note you write about it are stored with your account. Signed out, they stay on your device and we never see them.
  • Feedback you send from Settings — the message and its category. If you are signed in it is filed under your account; if not, it carries no identity at all.
  • Formulas you choose to publish — see "Shared formulas" below for exactly what a published formula contains, who can see it, and how to take it back.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera/photos, device advertising ID, or any analytics/usage tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs as of this policy's effective date. Two capabilities are built into the app but switched off in this release — photo upload and subscriptions — and the services behind them are named under "Third parties" below so that nothing in the app is undisclosed; this policy will be updated before either is switched on.

Shared formulas

You can choose to publish a formula so that other people can open it. Nothing is published unless you tap Publish, and the screen shows you exactly what will become visible before you confirm. A published formula contains: its name and preparation type, the herbs with their roles and proportions, the computed energetic, taste and action profile, any tags, the words you write for it (why you are sharing it, its flavour, what it is for, how to serve it), the notes you wrote on the formula — shown to you in full on the publish screen before you confirm, so you can edit them first — and a display name you choose, which may be left blank to publish anonymously. The notes are your own free text and are published only when you choose to share the formula. A published formula never contains your herb notebook, your email address or your account identity.

Who can see it. At present a published formula opens for anyone who has its link and the app. The app also has a community section, the Commons, where published formulas are listed for every reader to find; it is not yet open. When it opens, formulas that are published at that time are listed there, and the publish screen tells you this before you confirm. You can unpublish a formula at any time from your Library, after which its link no longer opens it and it is not listed.

Demonstration links. A formula published before 12 September 2026 may carry a link to a video of the preparation that its maker had posted on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Vimeo; the publish screen no longer offers this. Where such a link exists we store the address and nothing else. The app does not contact those services on your behalf or on a reader's; a reader who taps the link leaves the app and is then subject to that service's own privacy policy.

Reports and removal. Every published formula carries a Report button. Reports are read only by us, never by the person reported, and are reviewed by a person. A formula that is unsafe, unlawful, abusive, misleading about what a herb will do, or otherwise not what the app is for is hidden from everyone while it is reviewed and removed if the report is upheld; the account that published it is warned, and repeated or serious cases lead to the account being closed. There is no automated moderation and no third-party moderation service. If you believe something of yours was removed in error, contact us at the address below.

How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store and sync your saved formulas across your devices.
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. password reset, email confirmation) — we do not send marketing email.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Where your data is stored

Account and formula data is stored using Supabase, a backend infrastructure provider that hosts our database and handles authentication. Supabase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your saved formulas is restricted at the database level so that only your authenticated account can read or write them.

On your device, your sign-in session is encrypted and stored using your operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), not in plain text.

Supabase hosts our database in Northern Virginia, United States (AWS us-east-1). If you use the app from outside the United States, your account and formulas are transferred there in order to provide the service. For users in the UK or the European Economic Area, that transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses contained in Supabase's data processing addendum, which forms part of our agreement with them as our processor.

Third parties the app communicates with

These are every external service the app contacts. There are no others, and there is no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, acting as our processor. Receives your email address and your saved formulas. It does not use your data for its own purposes. (privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google tells us your email address, and Google knows that you signed in to this app. We ask Google for nothing else and send it nothing else. (privacy policy)
  • Google ML Kit face detection — built in, switched off in this release. When photo upload is enabled, it runs on your device to check a photo you attach for a human face and refuse it if one is found; the photo itself never leaves your device for that check. Google states that the library may report usage metrics to Google when it runs. It does not run while photo upload is off. (terms)
  • RevenueCat — built in, switched off in this release. When subscriptions are offered, it handles the purchase through Google Play and receives an app-generated identifier for your account and your purchase state; it never receives your email address. It is not contacted while subscriptions are off. (privacy policy)
  • Expo (EAS Update) — the app checks for a software update each time it launches. That request tells Expo your device platform, the app's version, and your IP address. It carries no account information, and it happens whether or not you are signed in. (privacy policy)
  • Google Play — distributes the app and may collect installation and crash diagnostics under its own terms, independently of anything the app does. (privacy policy)

How long we keep your data

Your account and formulas are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account — in the app, or by emailing us — the account and every formula saved to it are removed, and nothing is retained afterwards except where the law requires it. We keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider's routine backup rotation, which ages out within 30 days.

Why we are allowed to hold it

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the legal basis for processing your email address and your saved formulas is performance of a contract — you asked for an account and for your formulas to be stored, and neither is possible without them. We do not process your data on the basis of legitimate interests or consent for any other purpose, because there is no other purpose. You have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Your choices and rights

  • Access — you can view all your saved formulas at any time in the app's Library tab.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and all associated formulas at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Sign out — you can sign out of your account at any time from Settings without deleting your data.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can still request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at the address below from the email address associated with your account. State that you're requesting account deletion. We will delete your account and all saved formulas within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

Children's privacy

Herbalist's Companion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
