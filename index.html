Who we are

The app is operated by Broken Diamond P Ranch LLC, a company in Washington State, United States. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us about privacy at jared@penumbral.org.

Information we collect

We collect only what the app's features need.

Information you give us

  • Email address. We collect your email address when you create an account. We use it to sign you in and to let you reset your password.
  • Username. Before you publish a formula for the first time, you choose a username. It cannot be changed later, and it appears on every formula you publish.
  • Formulas you save. The herb formulas, notes, and preparation details you build and choose to save. Saving a formula requires an account, and a saved formula is stored with it. A formula you are still building on the bench stays on your device until you save it.
  • Your notes on herbs. If you are signed in, the marks you make on an herb (read, kept) and the note you write about it are stored with your account. If you are signed out, they stay on your device; on iPhone and iPad they are also included in your device's own backup if you use one, and on Android they are not. We never see them. If you later sign in on that device, the marks and notes you made there are moved into your account so they are not lost, as are any formulas an earlier version of the app saved on the device.
  • Formulas you publish, and their photos. See "Published formulas and what is public" for what a published formula contains, who can see it, and how to take it back.
  • Your agreement to the terms. The first time you publish a formula, the app shows the rules for shared formulas and asks you to agree to them. We keep a record that your account agreed, which version of the terms it was, and when, so we do not ask again until the terms change.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from Settings, we collect the message, its category, and the version of the app you sent it from. If you are signed in, it is filed under your account. If you are not, it carries no identity at all.
  • Reports. If you report a published formula, your report and the reason you give are filed under your account so that we can act on it.
  • Makers you hide. See "Reports, moderation, and hiding".

Information from sign-in services

  • Google. If you use "Continue with Google", Google gives us your email address. We ask Google for nothing else (not your name and not your photo), and we use the address only to create and identify your account.
  • Apple. If you use "Continue with Apple", Apple gives us your email address. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the relay address Apple gives us and never your real one. On iPhone and iPad we do not ask Apple for your name.
  • Your name, if you use "Continue with Apple" on Android and choose to share it. On Android, Apple's sign-in page shows the name on your Apple account and lets you change it before you continue. Whatever name you leave there is passed to us the first time you sign in this way and is stored with your account. The app does not show it or use it for anything, and it is deleted with your account.

Information collected automatically

  • Account identifier. A random number our database assigns to your account, which ties your saved work to you.
  • Server logs. Like any web service, our provider's servers record the IP address and time of each request the app makes to them, whether or not you are signed in. These logs are kept for a limited period for security and to diagnose faults. We do not use them to work out where you are or to build any profile of you.
  • Update checks. Each time the app launches, it asks our update service whether a newer update exists. See "Expo (EAS Update)" under "How we share your information" for what that request contains.
  • Records used to apply limits. To apply the app's limits on publishing and on photo checks, we record when each formula is first published and when each photo check is requested. Photo check records more than a day old are deleted each time a photo check is requested. The publishing record is deleted with your account.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or your device's advertising ID, and the app does no analytics or usage tracking. The app reads your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a formula you are publishing, and never otherwise. As of this policy's effective date, the app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Subscriptions are built into the app but switched off in this release. The subscription service is named under "How we share your information" so that nothing in the app is undisclosed, and we will update this policy before subscriptions are switched on.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • create and authenticate your account;
  • store and sync your saved formulas across your devices;
  • publish the formulas you choose to share, and show them to other people using the app;
  • check shared photos before anyone else can see them;
  • communicate with you about your account, such as password resets and email confirmation (we do not send marketing email);
  • read the feedback you send and fix what it reports; and
  • review reports about published formulas and remove what breaks our rules.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Legal bases for processing (UK and EEA)

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract for your email address and your saved formulas. You asked for an account and for your formulas to be stored, and neither is possible without them.
  • Your choice to send it for feedback.
  • Legitimate interests for reports and for the short-lived server logs described above, in keeping the service safe and working.
  • Performance of our agreement with you for publishing a formula and its photos, which we do at your request.
  • Legitimate interests for the automatic photo check, moderation records, and records of hidden makers, in keeping what is shared in the app safe and lawful.
  • Legitimate interests for the record of your agreement to the terms, in knowing who agreed to them and when.

You have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

  • Google Sign-In is used only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google tells us your email address, and Google knows that you signed in to this app. We ask Google for nothing else and send it nothing else. (Privacy policy)

When subscriptions are offered, it will handle the purchase through Google Play or the App Store and receive an app-generated identifier for your account and your purchase state.   • Apple (the App Store and TestFlight) distributes the app on iPhone and iPad and may collect installation and crash diagnostics under its own terms, where you have agreed to share them with developers. (Privacy policy)

Legal requirements. We may disclose information when the law requires it, including when we report unlawful content to the authorities.

Business transfers. If the app or our company is sold, merged, or otherwise transferred, the information we hold may pass to the new owner, who will be bound by the terms of this policy. If that happens, we will say so in the app or on this page.

Published formulas and what is public

You can choose to publish a formula so that other people can open it. Nothing is published unless you tap Publish, and the screen shows you exactly what will become visible.

What a published formula contains. Its name and preparation type, a tincture's alcohol percentage, the herbs with their roles and proportions, the computed energetic, taste, and action profile, any tags, the conditions you file it under, what you write for it on the publish screen (its description, its preparation instructions, and its Shared Notes), any photos you add, and your username. The private notes you keep on a formula in your Library are never published. A published formula never contains your herb notebook, your email address, or your account identity.

Who can see it. At present, anyone using the app can find a published formula. The Discover tab searches every published formula by the herbs in it, and anyone who has a formula's link can open it too. The publish screen says this before you confirm. The app also has a community section, the Commons, where published formulas are listed for every reader to find. It is not yet open. When it opens, formulas that are published at that time will be listed there, and the publish screen tells you this before you confirm.

Taking it back. You can unpublish a formula at any time from your Library. After that, its link no longer opens it and it is not listed.

Its history. When you change a published formula, the version it replaces is kept with it, so that its changes over time can be shown to you. Only you can read that history. It is deleted with the formula when you unpublish it or delete your account.

Photos. You may add up to six photos of plants, plant material, or the making to a formula you publish. The location and other camera details are removed from every photo on your device before it is uploaded. Photos are stored privately with your published formula and shown to people who open it. They are deleted when you remove them, when you unpublish the formula, or when you delete your account.

Demonstration links. A formula published before September 12, 2026 may carry a link to a video of the preparation that its maker had posted on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or Vimeo. The publish screen no longer offers this. Where such a link exists, we store the address and nothing else. The app does not contact those services on your behalf or on a reader's. A reader who taps the link leaves the app and is then subject to that service's own privacy policy.

Automated checks of shared photos

Every shared photo is checked automatically before anyone but you can see it. There is no other automated moderation.

On your device. Before a photo is added, your phone checks it for faces using the face detection built into your phone's own operating system (Apple's Vision framework on iPhone and iPad, and Android's own face detector on Android). That check runs entirely on your device, and the photo is not sent anywhere for it. A photo with a face, or one the phone cannot check, is not added.

On our server. When you choose or take a photo for a shared formula, and again after it is uploaded unless it was already checked when you chose it, our server sends it to Google Cloud Vision, a service that checks it on our behalf for sexual, violent, or otherwise explicit content and for a human face. The photo is sent to Google only for that check. Google's terms for this service say that the photo is processed in memory and not stored, that Google does not use it to train or improve its products, and that Google keeps a temporary log of some details about each request (not the photo) to run the service and prevent abuse (Cloud Vision data usage).

Faces. The face check only asks whether a face is present. It does not recognize or identify anyone, and we keep nothing about any face: no measurements, no outline, and no score.

What we keep. We keep Google's answer on explicit content and, if a photo is refused, the reason. When a photo passes as you choose it, we keep a short fingerprint of it. The fingerprint is a code worked out from the photo, which cannot be turned back into it, and not the photo itself. It lets the same photo pass after upload, within a day, without being sent to Google a second time. Fingerprints more than a day old are not used, and are deleted each time a photo check is requested.

A refused photo. A photo refused as you choose it is not kept by us at all and is not added to your formula. A photo refused after upload is deleted and taken off your formula. Either way, the app tells you so. Until the check passes, a photo is visible to you alone. If you believe a photo was refused in error, contact us at the address below.

Reports, moderation, and hiding

Reports. Every published formula carries a Report button for anyone signed in. Reports are read only by us, never by the person reported, and are reviewed by a person.

Removal and suspension. A formula that is unsafe, unlawful, abusive, misleading about what an herb will do, or otherwise not what the app is for is removed, with its photos. An account that publishes such a formula may be suspended, which stops it from signing in and publishing. Repeated or serious cases lead to the account being closed. We also review newly published formulas and their photos ourselves, without waiting for a report, and remove any that break these rules. If you believe something of yours was removed in error, contact us at the address below.

Hiding a formula or a maker. You can hide a published formula, or hide its maker so that none of their formulas appear for you. The maker is not told. A hidden formula is remembered on your device only. If you are signed in, the makers you hide are kept with your account, so that their formulas are left out before they reach your device, and that list entry is deleted when you show the maker again or delete your account. If you are signed out, the makers you hide are remembered on your device only.

Records of hides. Each time a signed-in reader hides a maker, we also keep a record of it by account number (the maker's and the reader's, with the time, and nothing else), so that we can see when several readers hide the same maker. That record is kept after the maker is shown again.

Moderation records. We keep a record of each moderation action we take, of each automatic photo refusal, and of each hide described above, by account number. These records hold no email address and no photo. They are not deleted when an account is deleted, so that a record of what was removed, and why, is not lost with it. We keep them for as long as we operate the app.

Data retention

We keep your account and formulas for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, in the app or by emailing us, the account and everything saved to it are removed: your formulas, your herb notes, the formulas you published and their photos, the record of your agreement to the terms, and the feedback and reports filed under your account.

After that, we keep only the moderation records described under "Reports, moderation, and hiding": records of moderation actions, of automatic photo refusals, and of hidden makers. They are kept by account number, with no email address and no photo, for as long as we operate the app.

Feedback sent while signed out carries no identity, so it cannot be traced to you or found to delete. Nothing deletes it automatically.

We keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider's routine backup rotation, which ages out within 30 days. Server logs are kept for a limited period, as described under "Information collected automatically".

Your choices and rights

  • Access. You can view all your saved formulas at any time in the app's Library tab.
  • Unpublish. You can unpublish any formula you have published at any time from your Library.
  • Hide. You can hide a published formula or its maker, and show a hidden maker again from Settings.
  • Sign out. You can sign out of your account at any time from Settings without deleting your data.
  • Delete. You can permanently delete your account and everything saved to it at any time. See "How to delete your account and data".
  • Other requests. To ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to exercise any other privacy right, contact us at the address below.

How to delete your account and data

In the app. While signed in, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If your account uses Sign in with Apple and you delete it on an iPhone or iPad, we also ask Apple to end the app's access to your Apple ID.

Without the app. If you no longer have the app installed, you can still request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at the address below from the email address associated with your account. State that you are requesting account deletion. We will delete your account and all saved formulas within 30 days.

What is deleted, and the little that is kept, is described under "Data retention".

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your saved formulas is restricted at the database level so that only your authenticated account can read or write them. On your device, your sign-in session is encrypted and stored using your operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain), not in plain text. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International data transfers

Supabase hosts our database in Ohio, United States (AWS us-east-2). If you use the app from outside the United States, your account and formulas are transferred there in order to provide the service. For users in the UK or the European Economic Area, that transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses contained in Supabase's data processing addendum, which forms part of our agreement with them as our processor.

Your rights wherever you live

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can delete your account in the app, or ask for deletion as described under "How to delete your account and data". For any other request, contact us at the address below. We do not sell your personal information.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising. It does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. Because the app does no such tracking, there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Children's privacy

Herbalist's Companion is made for adults, and you must be 18 or older to use it. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If it changes in a material way, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
